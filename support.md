## Frequently asked questions

**The AI doesn't answer.**
The default AI is Apple Intelligence. Check that it is turned on in *Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri* and that your iPad supports it. If it does not, you can:
- use the optional on-device model, on iPads with 8 GB of memory or more;
- or connect to your own DAFO Swarm computer.

See [How the AI works](ai-models.md).

**How long does the on-device model take to download?**
It is about 3.5 GB and downloads only once, in the background. You can keep using the App while it downloads, or cancel it at any time.

**How do I connect my iPad to DAFO Swarm?**
Install DAFO Swarm on your Mac, then enter its address in the App's Settings. The iPad and the Mac must be on the same network. See [How the AI works](ai-models.md#3-dafo-swarm).

**A lesson is greyed out.**
Steps unlock in order. You can still open a later step: the App will ask whether you want to try it anyway.

**Classroom mode doesn't find the teacher.**
Both iPads must be on the same Wi-Fi network, with Local Network permission allowed in *Settings → Mathsboard*.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open *Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions* on your iPad. Apple manages all subscriptions.

**How do I delete my data?**
Everything is stored on your iPad. Deleting the App deletes it.
