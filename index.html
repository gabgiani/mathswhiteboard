A tutor on your whiteboard

Everything happens where you write, with nothing to type.

Reads your handwriting

Write a formula or a whole exercise. Mathsboard turns it into maths and solves it step by step.

Explains every step

Each step comes with its reason, and you can ask questions about any of them.

Hints, not answers

Stuck? “I don't know what to do” shows only the next step. The result appears only if you ask.

Interactive explorers

Move a slider and watch the graph, the triangle or the neural network change as you learn.

Classroom mode

Teachers launch live challenges to students' iPads on the same network, with a podium and recorded explanations.

On the big screen

Show the board, the explanation or a lesson's introduction on an external display for the whole class.

Your choice of AI. Your data stays yours.

The worked steps come from Mathsboard's own maths engine, with no AI and no internet. For reading handwriting, explaining steps and giving hints, you choose one of three AI options.

Default

Apple Intelligence

Apple's language model, running on your iPad.

  • Runs oniPad
  • DownloadNone
  • Your data leaves the iPadNo

On-device model

Gemma 4 E2B on the iPad's chip with Apple's MLX. It works offline.

  • Runs oniPad (8 GB)
  • Download3.5 GB, once
  • Your data leaves the iPadNo

DAFO Swarm

An app for your own Mac that runs larger models and serves them to your iPad over your local network.

  • Runs onYour Mac
  • DownloadOn the Mac
  • Your data leaves the iPadOnly to your Mac
