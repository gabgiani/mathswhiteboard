For students

A path you can follow, one step at a time

Mathsboard doesn't hand you answers. It shows you the way, lets you write every step yourself and helps only as much as you need.

1

Create your profile

Four quick screens: student or teacher, your name and avatar, your level, and, if you like, your grade and goals.

The tutor uses them to adapt its vocabulary, depth and pace to you. Several students can share one iPad, each with their own profile and progress.

2

Choose what to learn

Four areas, 23 courses, from counting in Year 1 to the maths behind language models.

  • Each course is a path of lessons that unlock in order, so the screen is never overwhelming.
  • Want to jump ahead? You can: the app only suggests the recommended step first.
  • Every lesson starts with a short conceptual introduction you can listen to.
3

Learn on the whiteboard

The lesson's formula is already on the board, with an interactive explorer next to it and the worked explanation on the side.

  • Copy each step by hand with your Apple Pencil, following the explanation.
  • Move the sliders to see what each number changes.
  • Ask anything about a step in the chat, then tap Done to unlock the next lesson.
4

Practise and earn points

Every lesson ends with three exercises. Each one comes with its base formula and all the data you need, already written on the board.

  • Stuck? “I don't know what to do” shows only the next step; the result appears only if you ask.
  • Ask for similar exercises to any problem you write yourself.
  • Earn points, keep your streak and see real-world cases that use the same maths.
For teachers

Your class, on one board

Plan the lesson, teach on the whiteboard, and let every student follow and practise live on their own iPad. No internet needed: the classroom runs on the school's local network.

Plan, teach, follow up

  • Lesson plan: today's topics with their formulas; tick them off to know where to pick up next time.
  • Classroom whiteboard: entering it opens the room. Students join by scanning a QR code or typing the room code.
  • Live challenges: send an exercise to every iPad, watch the answers arrive and project the podium.
Recorded explanationsRecord your voice together with your strokes on the board. Students replay them in sync, step by step.

A tutor on your whiteboard

Everything happens where you write, with nothing to type. That works in the courses and on the free board, with any exercise you bring.

Reads your handwriting

Write a formula or a whole exercise. Mathsboard turns it into maths and solves it step by step.

Explains every step

Each step comes with its reason, and you can ask questions about any of them.

Interactive explorers

Move a slider and watch the graph, the triangle or the neural network change as you learn.

Your choice of AI. Your data stays yours.

The worked steps come from Mathsboard's own maths engine, with no AI and no internet. For reading handwriting, explaining steps and giving hints, you choose one of three AI options.

Default

Apple Intelligence

Apple's language model, running on your iPad.

  • Runs oniPad
  • DownloadNone
  • Your data leaves the iPadNo

On-device model

Gemma 4 E2B on the iPad's chip with Apple's MLX. It works offline.

  • Runs oniPad (8 GB)
  • Download3.5 GB, once
  • Your data leaves the iPadNo

DAFO Swarm

An app for your own Mac that runs larger models and serves them to your iPad over your local network.

  • Runs onYour Mac
  • DownloadOn the Mac
