# Privacy Policy — Mathsboard

**Last updated:** September 25, 2026 · [Versión en español](es/privacidad.md)

Mathsboard ("the App") is developed by Gabriel Giani Moreno. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the App.

**In short:** we do not collect your personal data, we have no servers that receive your work, and there are no ads, analytics or trackers.

## 1. What stays on your iPad

The following is stored only on your device:

- your whiteboards, handwriting and formulas;
- student profiles (name, avatar, level, grade, country and goals);
- course progress and settings.

We never receive it. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it.

## 2. Artificial intelligence

The App offers three AI options. You choose one in Settings. The details are in [How the AI works](ai-models.md).

| Option | Where your exercise is processed | Leaves your iPad? |
|---|---|---|
| **Apple Intelligence** (default) | On your iPad, with Apple's on-device model | No |
| **On-device model** (optional) | On your iPad, with an open model you download once | Only the one-time model download from Hugging Face. Your exercises never leave the iPad. |
| **DAFO Swarm** (optional) | On a computer **you** run and control, usually your own Mac on your local network | Yes, to that computer only. It never reaches us. |

We do not operate any AI server. Your exercises are never sent to us.

## 3. Features that use a network

- **Classroom mode.** It uses Apple's local peer-to-peer networking (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth) to connect a teacher's iPad with students' iPads in the same room. The following are shared only between those devices:
  - challenges and answers;
  - names shown in the classroom;
  - recorded explanations (voice and strokes).
- **Global Community ranking (optional).** If you open this tab, the App publishes a leaderboard entry through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit): a random pseudonymous alias, avatar, XP, streak and level. It never includes a real name, age, country, drawings or audio.
- **Course updates.** The App may download updated course content from Apple's iCloud. Nothing about you is uploaded.
- **Purchases.** Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

## 4. Permissions

- **Camera:** to scan a classroom QR code and to photograph an exercise. Photos are processed on the device or by the AI option you chose.
- **Microphone:** only when a teacher records a voice explanation. The recording is stored on the iPad and shared only with that teacher's class.
- **Local network:** only for Classroom mode and for DAFO Swarm.

## 5. Tracking and advertising

The App contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

## 6. Children

The App is designed for students of all ages, including children. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The only data that can leave the device is described above. It is never used for advertising or profiling.
